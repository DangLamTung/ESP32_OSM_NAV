OSM Nav — Web Bluetooth → ESP32-S3 map

service 5a7e1000 · NAV-OSM

🚀 One-click navigation

One button sends all the BLE the board needs — route (first 64-point batch), position at the route start, next-turn nav, clock and ETA — then drives the full route. Auto-connects first if needed.

1 · Route

2 · Drive simulation (follows the route path)

Interpolates the vehicle along the full route and sends <pos> packets ~10×/s (kept low so the board's BLE/redraw stays stable). The board interpolates + extrapolates, so the map scrolls continuously. Long roads are streamed in 64-point batches that advance as you drive. × is a time-lapse multiplier — lower it if the movement looks too fast.

3 · Maneuver + position (manual)

4 · Time + ETA (HUD)

Current time (<clock>) and ETA (<eta>) shown in the board's top HUD.

5 · Weather (HUD)

Sends a <weather> packet shown in the board's weather widget (top-right). During a drive it's sent once with the navigation start.

6 · Speed camera (HUD alert)

Sends a 0x09 camera packet; the board shows an amber CAMERA nnn m alert while the car is near a camera. Dist 0 clears it. During a drive the sim auto-announces a camera ahead every ~3 km so you can watch it count down.

Connect

Needs Chrome/Edge. Serve this page over https or localhost (Web Bluetooth requires a secure context) — e.g. python -m http.server 8000 then open http://localhost:8000.
